# Combat Clarity and Recipe Filters — 0.3.24

The defender draft occupies 209 px on desktop and 300 px on narrow screens. Narrow screens use a horizontal card strip; Keep and Merge remain separate 44 px actions. Pause, Resume and speed sit beside Enemy Waves and remain visible during combat.

The unselected command panel shows the current assault: enemies still alive plus queued arrivals, effective maximum HP, armor, movement speed, resistances and abilities. Profiles aggregate actual combat instances and queued variant choices without revealing hidden enemy positions. Selecting a defender or revealed invader retains its individual inspection. A draft defender's paid downgrade appears immediately below its portrait and acts on its actual field rank.

Ranger, Master Ranger, Dark Ranger and Ranger Oathbreaker bypass all damage defenses of airborne enemies, including armor, magic and elemental resistance, wards, both damage immunities, evasion, hit-blocking shields and Kraken Shell. Shields are bypassed without consumption. Ground enemies keep their normal defenses. Concealed enemies must still be revealed before targeting. Anti-air support effects follow the same immunity exception. Other units' rules are unchanged.

Actual damage from attacks, poison, fire auras, splash, lightning and other damaging abilities feeds the owning defender's peak DPS. Applied damage is capped at the target's remaining HP for this measurement. Strongest poison applies without stacking: each source keeps its own expiry, and a weaker source does not extend a stronger source's lifetime. The remaining weaker application takes over when the stronger application expires. Nature Spirit now applies 96 poison damage/s; Mother Nature applies 1,898.4/s. Their direct damage, intervals, range and support remain unchanged. These are explicit current campaign overrides; historical source definitions are preserved.

A genuine critical hit displays a small red **Crit!** above its impact. The label comes from successful damage after defenses, never a separate random roll. Misses, blocked hits and immune hits cannot produce it. It follows battle time, pauses with combat, respects concealment and reduced motion, and expires after 0.85 game seconds. The last killing critical can finish its label during the next construction phase.

Recipes filters combine damage type, ability and enemy defense. They inspect actual rank statistics and all damaging channels, including secondary pure splash and damaging auras. Matching ally support and conditional/chance abilities are included and explained. Ranger defense bypass is explicitly limited to flying targets. Basic rank rows and champion recipe cards are filtered together; resetting restores all 86 profiles. Filters persist while pinning recipes.

Baby Fire Dragon receives a uniform presentation factor of 2.3, placing its actual approved-model height within 3% of Archer I. Mother Fire Dragon, Elemental Male Dragon and Ancient Dragon Patriarch receive factors of 1.25, 1.2 and 1.15 respectively. Their actual heights increase through the recipe chain. Classification scaling stays separate, with a factor of 1.1 per class step; auras, wall foundations, approved GLB geometry and original voice files retain their existing identities.

Run results display their original game version. Online rankings combine all versions of the same 10- or 50-wave campaign and retain exact-version API queries for older clients. No table is recreated and no existing score is rewritten. Local best scores and run history use stable keys and preserve legacy records without inventing an unknown version. Repeated reporting of one run ID cannot downgrade its finalized score.
