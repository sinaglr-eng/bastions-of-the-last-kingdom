# FR-006 and FR-007 revision 3 — current implementation

Version **0.6.11**, ruleset **fr006-fr007-r3-20261106**. The user authorized implementation of the latest recipe revision and public deployment on 6 November 2026. The exact requirement drafts and handoffs remain historical evidence.

FR-006 makes a surviving final boss crossing the exit end a 50-wave run immediately in defeat. All three forms use the same rule in every faction. The leak consumes the health actually present at that instant; statistics record that value. No queued hit, healing proc, wave-completion score, MVP award or subsequent update can turn that defeat into a win. Earlier editions, other bosses and the 10-wave contract retain their prior rules. Backend checkpoint validation distinguishes the new edition without deleting or migrating player records.

Wave 49 now restores **15% of missing health every four simulation seconds**. A guardian at 20/100 HP reaches 32 and then 42.2 HP. The existing simulation clock, speed controls, pause, healing block and reduction semantics are preserved. The guide and inspection derive their text from the effective enemy definition. Original authoring data remains unchanged.

The current presentation includes the subsequent user size feedback: all 258 V3 defenders receive a uniform 20% reduction, all 21 Basic-class champions are capped at their own ordinary draft reference, and the Human class ladder now starts at the draft median with the established 10% class steps. The original FR-006 draft targets remain historical measurement evidence; the current targets and exception rules are documented in [Current battlefield defender sizing](DEFENDER_PRESENTATION.md).

Ballista, Catapult, Fire Ballista, Cannon, Royal Battery and Golem are explicit low, broad chassis/construct exceptions: their longest body footprint uses the corresponding class reference rather than inflating them to standing height. They retain their authored proportions. Draft ranks, Dwarf and Elf figures and shared Lady Claire also receive the common 20% reduction. GLBs, geometry, materials, clips, combat ranges and collision rules are unchanged. Native Idle/Attack checks now cover all 258 approved profiles. Original FR-006 same-camera comparisons remain verification evidence for that implementation, while the current size-feedback review covers the new presentation.

The finished run’s faction is visible directly below its score. Save forms, selected standings and save confirmations identify faction, version and campaign. A player's row outside the Top 10 also identifies its faction. Browsing earlier versions or Legacy records does not alter the completed run identity; missing historical attribution is shown as “Faction not recorded.” Wrong-faction or wrong-campaign responses fail verification before rendering.

The earlier FR-007 revision 2 implementation applied 47 recipe arrays. The current **revision 3 adds the exact 16 latest recipe changes** relative to 0.6.10, affecting 51 recursive material totals. Seven of eight rank-I basics in each new faction have one immediate output; Blacksmith I and Stormspear I each have two. Higher classes directly add basic ranks II or above. Every basic rank retains a recipe and every profile has at most three outputs. All 258 combat profiles, approved assets and Human/shared Secret recipes are unchanged. All 74 ordinary recipes, predecessor links, branch pools and rank ceilings remain verified. All 16 TOP recipes require an Advanced predecessor plus two rank-VI basics. Nine terminal Advanced recipes use an external family; Mortar Dwarf retains its selected Dwarf Sniper + Musketeer IV + Grenadier IV exception.

Real game crafting tests consume three distinct ingredient instances for each of the 74 recipes, including repeated family/rank ingredients. Rank VI still requires two fresh rank-V rolls from the same completed draft. These checks establish recipe reachability and material accounting; they do not certify the balance or success of a full legal 50-wave campaign.

The single English gallery remains local only, with current implementation metadata and the original proposal preserved separately. Original artwork, prompts, appearance decisions, local models and score records are preserved. No gallery or model files were published.

Current release verification is in `output/implementation/release-current/`; earlier required FR-006 and sizing regression evidence is retained in its original implementation folders. Tests cover final-boss transitions, healing, historical score cohorts, all 74 recipes, all latest successor lists and all 258 native defender models. Browser and automated checks do not replace physical iPad hardware testing.
