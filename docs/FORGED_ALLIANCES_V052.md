# Forged Alliances TD — 0.5.2

Wave 49 (Troll Guardians of the Deep Maw) now restores **10% of missing health every four seconds**, reduced from 25%. A guardian with 100 maximum HP and 20 current HP reaches 28 HP after the first pulse and 35.2 HP after the second. Healing block and pulse timing are unchanged. Other waves retain their current values. The guide derives its 10% label from the active combat profile.

The TD letters in the wordmark increase from 25 to 50 SVG units, with a taller seal. The emblem and other lettering retain their proportions. Both the game header and initial loading screen use logo revision 2; original revision 1 is preserved.

No gallery, original unit asset, statistics API or archive changes are included.
