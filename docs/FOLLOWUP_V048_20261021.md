This update addresses the two browser comments and six follow-up requests on the published game: clearer planning and wave controls, automatic completed-goal removal, late-wave model safety, visible result saving, a guided first wave, hand-cast lightning, and more varied defeat artwork.

**Deployment pending.** The final local gate passes **1,738 of 1,738 tests** and the **213-module** production build. Local native browser review passes the full tutorial on phone and tablet viewports. Pages CI, public-file verification and guarded MAIN delivery remain separate gates; this initial report does not claim publication.

## Tutorial behavior and responsive correction

The guide observes ordinary actions; it never places recruits, chooses a keeper or merge, changes transport values, spends resources or consumes simulation RNG. Placement and commitment still use the existing legality checks. Opening a book temporarily hides coaching and cannot satisfy the Pause task through the book's modal pause. Keyboard focus inside the guide does not also drive the battlefield; native buttons and the instructions disclosure retain normal keyboard/touch activation. Ending restores a visible game control. New runs and ended/replaced games clear coaching without falsely recording completion.

Replay during blueprint editing shows an explicit explanation and a focused **Return to blueprint** control. Unsaved cells and editor history remain intact. An uncommitted Move cancels without spending CP or moving a defender before replay starts. Developer setup replacement avoids a repeated ordinary-run invitation.

The original phone review found a real blocker: a 350 × 269-pixel placement card covered almost the entire exposed map between the header and recruit folio. The [initial capture](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/02-tutorial-place-phone.jpg) is retained. The correction uses a compact task card on narrow or short screens, a default-collapsed native **Show instructions** disclosure, an accessible End action and no wide disabled Next button while waiting. It accounts for the folio's actual top edge, leaves the lower map available and keeps Start/Pause/Speed above their explanation. Desktop instructions remain expanded. The preceding local gate and focused receipts are preserved as `*-before-mobile-fix` and `*-pre-mobile` evidence.

Native desktop Chromium review of the final local build exercised:

- **390 × 844:** all five real map placements, reachable Keep, both books and their close actions, Start, Pause, Speed 2× and Finish tutorial. Reload again offered Start/Skip. The [corrected placement view](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/03-tutorial-place-phone-fixed.jpg), [keeper view](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/04-tutorial-keeper-phone-fixed.jpg) and [speed view](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/06-tutorial-speed-phone-fixed.jpg) retain the observed layout.
- **1,024 × 768:** five real placements, Keep, Start, Pause, Speed and Finish. The fixed **2× → 3× → 5× → 1×** cycle also passed. [Placement](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/08-tutorial-place-tablet.jpg) and [completed guide](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/09-tutorial-completed-tablet.jpg) captures record the tablet viewport.

These are responsive desktop browser viewports, not a physical iPad/Safari certification. The [independent review](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/tutorial-independent-review.json) preserves the initial replay finding and its later read-only recheck.

## Late-wave models and hand lightning

The [render proof](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/render/render-proof.json) reproduces missing requests for `host_31-ember`, `host_35-ember`, `host_36-ember`, `host_50-tyrant` and `host_50-devourer`. These combat variants now resolve to their approved solid source model. The four explicitly authored spectral variants retain their separate GLB identities. Queue/next-wave loading uses the same canonical identity mapping.

Cache protection includes actual live figures, corpses and the camp preview until their private actors are removed. This prevents an otherwise valid source template and its texture images from being released while a scene borrower still uses them. Tests retain exact embedded PNG bindings, original materials and usable finite lighting normals at late animation clocks. Passive context-loss/restoration counters are exposed only through the existing developer diagnostics; they do not prevent browser events or change Three.js recovery, rendering quality or simulation.

The [local wave-35 observation](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/current-048-wave35.json) records approved solid/spectral sources, no pending enemy load, a colored visible paused enemy and zero observed context-loss events. It does not reproduce the reported fully black appearance, identify a device-specific driver cause or prove sustained performance. Physical Safari remains unverified.

Stormcaller uses its right-hand release surface. Elven Mage and Elven Archmage use the approved left casting hand, rather than a staff/orb surface. Each lightning frame reads the posed source anchor; existing target endpoints, shot progress and resolved chain/fork outcomes remain authoritative. Actual-GLB regression tests establish numeric emitter-to-hand equality. Native [Stormcaller](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/10-stormcaller-hand-lightning.jpg) and [Archmage](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/11-archmage-hand-lightning.jpg) captures check the visible discharge; screenshots alone do not measure emitter coordinates.

The [result-layout receipt](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/results-default-open.json) exercises the actual production result and leaderboard markup in a local read-only fixture. Online scores is open without fixture forcing, and the name form appears before Top 10. The [capture](../output/design/followup-v048-20261021/native/12-results-default-open.jpg) records that layout. The fixture does not instantiate the statistics client or write a score.
