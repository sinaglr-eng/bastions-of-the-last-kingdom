Player accounts & privacy

Updated 14 November 2026 · Forged Alliances TD 0.7.0

Playing without an account

You can play as a Guest. A Guest continuation and preferences stay in this browser. Guest runs do not create account medals or public leaderboard entries.

Signing in

When enabled, Google, Apple or Facebook authenticates your existing account and shares a provider-specific identifier, your email address and basic profile information. We request identity scopes only. The game does not request access to your Gmail inbox, Google Drive, contacts or Apple purchases. Your provider password is entered only on that provider's sign-in page.

A player profile is created on your first successful sign-in. A short public nickname is suggested automatically; change it in Account. Use the same sign-in provider on other devices to retrieve the same progress. Apple's Hide My Email can give your account a relay email address.

When email registration is enabled, the game stores a password hash, verifies your email with a link and supports password recovery through expiring single-use email links. It never stores your readable password. Facebook identities whose email ownership is unverified must complete email verification before accessing progress.

Information the game stores

  • Account identifier, email, public nickname and linked sign-in provider identifiers.
  • Saved games, validated game actions, scores, achievements, medal transactions and General equipment.
  • Authentication sessions, including expiration, IP address and browser information used by the authentication service.
  • Provider tokens needed for the sign-in connection, encrypted by the game's authentication service.

Your nickname, score, faction and starting General equipment appear in Hall of Fame for submitted verified results. Your email, provider identifiers, session tokens and private saves are not part of the public standings.

Cookies and local storage

Necessary secure, HttpOnly cookies maintain hosted sign-in sessions for up to 14 days. Short-lived state cookies protect sign-in redirects. Browser storage keeps preferences, saved continuation and pending game actions for offline play. The game does not include advertising or third-party analytics scripts. Hosting services may retain operational request logs under their own policies.

Storage and removal

Account records are stored in the game's persistent backend database. Progress stays available until you delete your player account or the service is discontinued. In Account, “Delete player account” removes this game's account, saves, scores, achievements, medal ledger and linked provider records. It requires a recent sign-in and explicit confirmation. This does not delete your Google, Apple or Facebook account. Guest saves and browser preferences can be removed with your browser's site-data controls.

For game bugs, use the project issue tracker. Do not post private emails, passwords, tokens or keys there. Google, Apple and Meta manage their own account privacy and consent settings.
