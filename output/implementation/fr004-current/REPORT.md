# FR-004 implementation and balance validation

The local candidate is game **0.6.7**, ruleset **fr004-r1-20261101**. Exact numeric implementation and campaign completion are separate conclusions. No hosted publication was requested.

## Exact implementation

All 85 native Dwarf primary fields match the implementation handoff: 80 at 1.5 times baseline and five machines at 2 times baseline. Four continuous weapons use effective DPS once; canonical shared Flame Cone levels and fixed secondary values remain unchanged. All ten Physical immunity aura owners retain their levels and fractions with 1.5 tiles more reach. Human, Elves, shared Claire, recipes, timing, attack reach and approved media remain unchanged. All 2,008 tests and the 226-module local build passed.

## Fresh legal Diamond Spiral campaigns

Twelve frozen fresh initial seeds × two fixed policies × three factions produced 72 legal current campaigns. Another 24 paired Dwarf baseline campaigns use the exact FR-003 regression registry. The controller and construction plan were frozen before collection. All actions use normal game APIs and resource costs, ordinary rank VI is allowed, and TOP/Secret are excluded. No army, walls, resources, ranks, favorable draws or MVP were injected. Natural Claire eligibility is recorded separately.

Completed mazes use 128 Diamond foundations and 718 ground-route steps; incomplete construction and airborne checkpoint routes remain real. Raw action records include lost waves, health/leaks, genuine boss deaths, craft timing and natural Secret eligibility. Exact/abstract duplicate histories are flagged in the JSON summary rather than treated as independent samples.

## Controlled late waves

117 real-engine cases use the same fixed 600-material armies: waves 27, 35, 40, 43, 45, 46, 48, 49 and 50, every final-boss form, and selected half-timestep checks. These injected armies test capacity, not legal acquisition. Genuine boss deaths and full clears are counted independently from surviving a boss escape. The fixed Dwarf army still leaks the final boss in its 0.1-second cases; the selected 0.05-second seed clears it. This timestep sensitivity and remaining boss bottleneck are retained in the report without further parameter changes.
